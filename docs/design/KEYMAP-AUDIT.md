A rule's `when` is its `kbExpr` and the command's `precondition`. Contexts that always hold while the focused editor is in its default state are implicit; the contexts quire's `When` models (`editorHasMultipleSelections`, `findWidgetVisible`, the suggest widget's and snippet mode's) are kept, joined with `&&`; a rule with any other context is N/A (listed with the reason).
